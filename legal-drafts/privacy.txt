1. Introduction and controller
At Uniify for Artists, we are committed to protecting the privacy, security and personal information of our users. This policy explains how we collect, process, store, share and protect information across our digital services. We aim to comply with UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018 and applicable legislation. Current data controller: Samuel Fakunle, trading as Uniify for Artists. Contact: uniifyforartists@gmail.com. Contact location: United Kingdom.

2. Information we collect
Account information: name, artist name, email, country or general location, preferences and authentication information.
Career information: genre, stage, experience, biography, goals, catalogue, releases, audience and streaming figures, profile links, promotional budgets and development needs.
Platform activity: assessment responses, roadmaps, recommendations, tasks, release plans, milestones and progress.
Technical information, where collected: IP address, device/browser details, login activity, usage, security logs, cookies and browser storage. Please do not submit unnecessary sensitive information or financial account credentials.

3. How information is collected
Information is collected through website visits, registration, profile updates, assessments, tasks, releases, platform interactions and support correspondence. Before introducing external music integrations, we will explain what information they supply.

4. Purposes and lawful bases
Contract: provide accounts, assessments, personalised roadmaps, release planning, recommendations and progress tracking.
Legitimate interests: security, fraud prevention, service reliability and improvement, subject to users’ rights and freedoms.
Consent: optional marketing and non-essential cookies where required.
Legal obligation: comply with applicable laws and regulatory requirements.

5. Personalisation
We may analyse career stage, goals, budget, release history, completed activities and development needs to recommend relevant actions. Guidance is advisory; users remain in control. Before introducing external AI processing, we will explain its purpose, data involved and safeguards.

6. Sharing and international transfers
We do not sell personal information. We may share necessary information with authorised hosting, database, authentication, email, infrastructure and technical support providers, or authorities where legally required. Appropriate contractual safeguards must apply. For overseas transfers, we will use applicable UK adequacy or approved contractual safeguards. Details may be requested by email. Additional privacy information will precede payment, AI, music integrations or marketplace processing.

7. Security and retention
We aim to use appropriate technical and organisational safeguards, including secure authentication, access controls and secure transmission. No online service guarantees absolute security. We retain information only as long as necessary for the stated purposes. Registration details are retained for six months from registration. You may request earlier deletion by contacting us. Other records, including backups, security logs and support correspondence, are retained only as long as necessary for their stated purposes or to meet legal obligations.

8. Your rights and account deletion
Depending on circumstances, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, withdrawal of consent or object to processing. You may object to direct marketing at any time and to legitimate-interest processing where applicable. Contact uniifyforartists@gmail.com to exercise rights or request account deletion. We will respond within statutory time limits.

9. Cookies and marketing
Cookies and browser storage may support authentication, security, preferences and functionality. Where consent is required, we will request it before activating non-essential technologies and allow preferences to be changed. Optional marketing may include updates, resources and opportunities; users can unsubscribe at any time. Essential service communications are separate.

10. Children, complaints and changes
Uniify is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly register under-18s. Contact us about privacy concerns or complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk; 0303 123 1113). We will communicate material policy changes where appropriate.
